## Expertise That Spans the Full Stack

The Team at Hella Labs brings together battle-tested expertise from Web2 enterprise systems, Silicon Valley startups, and cutting-edge Web3 protocols. Our engineers have shipped production code at **ConsenSys**, **Stellar**, and **Uphold**—organizations at the forefront of blockchain infrastructure.

**What sets us apart:**

- **12+ years** of distributed systems and cloud-native architecture experience
- **Active open-source contributors** with tools used by development teams worldwide
- **Technical educators** creating courses for LinkedIn Learning and Pluralsight that have taught thousands of developers
- **Conference speakers** at ETH Denver, Devcon, and ETH CC, sharing knowledge with the global Web3 community
- **Polkadot Blockchain Academy** graduates bringing formal blockchain education and best practices

We don't just build applications—we build the **testing infrastructure** and **developer tools** that make Web3 development reliable and scalable. Our open-source projects like [Foundry Anvil Testcontainers](https://github.com/hella-web3/foundryanvil-testcontainers-nodejs) and [WireMock Testcontainers](https://github.com/hella-web3/wiremock-testcontainers-nodejs) demonstrate our commitment to raising the bar for quality in blockchain development.
