In Web3, a bad release isn't a rollback. It's an exploit, a drained treasury, or an immutable bug
that stays on-chain for good. We treat **CI/CD and test automation as a risk management
framework**: find where your protocol can fail, measure how likely and how costly each failure is,
and put automated controls in front of every one of them.

For a startup competing with entrenched players, that framework also works as a force multiplier.
Your team ships faster because every merge is checked against the risks that matter.

## Our Framework

1. **Identify:** We audit your contracts, dApp, backend services, and pipeline to map every point
   where a change can break production.
2. **Assess:** Each risk is ranked by likelihood and blast radius, so automation effort goes where
   a failure would cost the most.
3. **Control:** We build automated controls: isolated E2E suites on disposable chains, mainnet-fork
   tests, mocked external dependencies, and gated CI/CD pipelines.
4. **Monitor:** Controls run on every commit. Test reports, coverage, and pipeline metrics show
   whether your risk exposure is going up or down.

## Proven in Open Source

We don't just recommend this tooling. We build and maintain it. Our open-source Testcontainers
modules are the same controls we deploy for clients:

- [**Foundry Anvil Testcontainers**](/open-source/foundry-anvil-testcontainers/) manages
  **smart contract and environment risk**. It spins up isolated, reproducible Anvil nodes per test
  suite, with mainnet forking, account impersonation, and time travel for DeFi, governance, and
  exploit-reproduction scenarios.
- [**WireMock Testcontainers**](/open-source/wiremock-testcontainers/) manages **integration
  risk**. It replaces RPC providers and third-party APIs with containerized, verifiable mocks, so
  you can test outages, timeouts, and malformed responses on demand.

Together they let you run [on-chain chaos engineering](/on-chain-chaos-engineering-ethereum-wiremock-anvil/):
deliberately injecting failures to prove your protocol degrades safely.

### Quality You Can Verify

machine passes on every machine, so results are **reproducible by anyone**: your team, your
